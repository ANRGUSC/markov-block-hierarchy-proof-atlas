| Comparison | Matched design | Decision it can inform |
|---|---|---|
| Two parallel authors versus one combined proof | D1–D3. Two authors receive separate conjuncts. A third receives the conjunction. The split path additionally builds a declaration with exactly the combined type. | Do two available workers finish the parent earlier after claiming, compilation and the join? |
| Two sequential subproofs versus a combined proof | P1–P3. One author per arm. Treat each subproof as a separately checked unit, including an assembled parent. Alternate the author assigned the intervention. | Does reducing goal size save writing or debugging time at fixed headcount? |
| Sketch first versus draft directly | P4–P6. One author writes a short mathematical sketch on the clock. The other may draft immediately. | When does planning pay for its preparation cost? This question is motivated by [Draft, Sketch, and Prove](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.12283). |
| Retrieve premises first versus search on demand | P7–P9. One author looks up relevant declarations in the pinned mathlib before drafting and records the queries. Both may use the same local library. | Do fewer misapplied lemmas and retries repay the lookup time? [LeanDojo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15626) motivates this comparison. |
| Lamport hierarchy versus equal-content prose | P10–P12. Both authors receive the same mathematical hints. The hierarchy labels each step and lists its dependencies. The prose arm receives the same sentences in the same order. | Does exposing the proof structure improve authoring speed or localize the first failed check? The outlines are prepared once and are reusable. Separately record the preparation cost when an outline is produced for one arm alone. [Lamport](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/proof.pdf) |
| Related verified proof supplied versus ordinary access | Select three fresh obligations with a genuine earlier analogue. Name the analogue and its structural relationship before starting. Record its size and the time to select it. Both authors receive identical imports and may search the repository. Only one receives the related proof as initial context. | When do transfer and adaptation outweigh reading time? Record any misleading similarity and spontaneous discovery in the control. |
| Up-front versus triggered decomposition | Select three more substantial obligations with separable intermediate facts. Predeclare the trigger, such as two failed checks on the same subgoal or three elapsed authoring minutes without a complete candidate. Log whether it fired, the failure and the split boundary. Hold headcount fixed. | Which goal features or stall signals justify splitting, and where do checks and joins dominate? |
| Schedule independent lemmas as soon as ready | For three later batches, freeze a small dependency graph from the paper. Time a serial author and a parallel team on identical deliverables. Record readiness, idle intervals, the longest dependency chain and the check of the dependent parent. | Can scheduling accelerate the parent? Compare ready-work time and total author effort. |

## Recording and interpretation

Freeze the Lean propositions, imports, strategy assignment and eligible earlier context before revealing them. Keep author files isolated and disallow sharing solutions between arms. Both arms must compile the same contract under the pinned toolchain, with axiom reports limited to the project's accepted foundational axioms. Keep the exact source, successes, failed attempts, start times and finish times. Record known prior exposure, dependency changes or a busy worker. For a fair stand-alone comparison use a common readiness gate. Label pipelined races separately.

Use release-to-parent-certificate as the primary practical outcome. Report time to the first submitted proof, Lean subprocess time, time outside Lean including retries, author occupancy and assembly costs separately. Outside-Lean wall time includes tool and scheduling delays. Record the cost of supplying a sketch, hint or retrieved proof when comparing end-to-end approaches. Keep one-variable comparisons separate from changes in headcount.

After three matched pairs, inspect the direction, effect size, failures and goal types individually. A working heuristic should have a stated scope and a counterexample or stopping condition. Treat mixed results as unresolved and retest on a different claim. Compare more substantial goals before extrapolating from calculations. Revisit surviving heuristics prospectively on unseen proofs to see if they predict which approach will finish first.
